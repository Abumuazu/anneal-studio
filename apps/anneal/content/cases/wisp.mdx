<Brief msg="Three questions came first." width="wide">
  Boards, committees and leadership teams make decisions that have to be recorded correctly. They
  needed to capture meetings on a phone, in the room or on a Teams or Zoom call, without losing
  minutes to a locked screen or a bad connection. Every meeting also carries a classification, and a
  draft only becomes the official record once a secretary approves it. We designed and built the
  whole app.
</Brief>

<Questions
  msg="Three questions came first."
  heading="Three questions we answered before drawing a screen."
>
  <Question title="What happens when the phone locks mid-meeting?">
    Recording keeps going. A Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the
    timer and level meter, and a long press opens pause and stop.
  </Question>
  <Question title="How do classification and consent get captured before recording?">
    Every meeting carries a classification: Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. The person
    recording confirms that everyone present has been told the session is recorded.
  </Question>
  <Question title="How does a transcript become an official record?">
    Only after review. The recording is uploaded to the organization's own servers and transcribed
    with speaker labels. Draft minutes then go to a secretary, who can suggest edits and approve
    them.
  </Question>
</Questions>

<WispFlow msg="Lock the phone, keep recording." heading="Follow one meeting from start to minutes.">
  <FlowStep title="Start a capture">
    Set the classification, confirm that everyone present has been told, and start recording.
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Lock the phone">
    Recording continues when the screen locks or you switch apps. A Live Activity keeps the timer on
    the lock screen.
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Dynamic Island">
    The compact view shows a level meter and timer. When another app shares the island, it shrinks
    to a minimal view.
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Long press for controls">
    Press and hold to open the full controls: pause, resume and stop. The lock screen uses the same
    layout, so it reads as one object.
  </FlowStep>
  <FlowStep title="Stop, saved, minutes">
    The recording is saved and uploaded to the organization's own servers. A transcript with speaker
    labels follows in about 4 minutes, then draft minutes for a secretary to review.
  </FlowStep>
</WispFlow>

<Principle msg="Follow one meeting through.">
  If recording stops when the phone locks, nobody trusts the minutes.
</Principle>

<Features

  <Feature title="Capture anywhere">

  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Works offline">
    Recordings stay safe on the phone and sync once it is back online.
  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Classification and consent">

  <Feature title="Live Activity">

  <Feature title="One view per meeting">

  </Feature>
  <Feature title="Review before record">
    Draft minutes a secretary reviews, edits and approves. Alerts tell people when a transcript is
    ready or an action lands.
  </Feature>
</Features>
