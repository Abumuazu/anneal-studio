gov.brief: Boards, committees and leadership teams make decisions that have to be recorded correctly. They needed to capture meetings on a phone, in the room or on a Teams or Zoom call, without losing minutes to a locked screen or a bad connection. Every meeting also carries a classification, and a draft only becomes the official record once a secretary approves it. We designed and built the whole app.
gov.q.h2: Three questions we answered before drawing a screen.
gov.q.1.t: What happens when the phone locks mid-meeting?
gov.q.1.x: Recording keeps going. A Live Activity on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island shows the timer and level meter, and a long press opens pause and stop.
gov.q.2.t: How do classification and consent get captured before recording?
gov.q.2.x: Every meeting carries a classification: Public, Internal, Confidential or Restricted. The person recording confirms that everyone present has been told the session is recorded.
gov.q.3.t: How does a transcript become an official record?
gov.q.3.x: Only after review. The recording is uploaded to the organization's own servers and transcribed with speaker labels. Draft minutes then go to a secretary, who can suggest edits and approve them.
gov.flow.h2: Follow one meeting from start to minutes.
gov.flow.1.t: Start a capture
gov.flow.1.x: Set the classification, confirm that everyone present has been told, and start recording.
gov.flow.2.t: Lock the phone
gov.flow.2.x: Recording continues when the screen locks or you switch apps. A Live Activity keeps the timer on the lock screen.
gov.flow.3.t: Dynamic Island
gov.flow.3.x: The compact view shows a level meter and timer. When another app shares the island, it shrinks to a minimal view.
gov.flow.4.t: Long press for controls
gov.flow.4.x: Press and hold to open the full controls: pause, resume and stop. The lock screen uses the same layout, so it reads as one object.
gov.flow.5.t: Stop, saved, minutes
gov.flow.5.x: The recording is saved and uploaded to the organization's own servers. A transcript with speaker labels follows in about 4 minutes, then draft minutes for a secretary to review.
gov.principle: If recording stops when the phone locks, nobody trusts the minutes.
gov.built.h2: What we built.
gov.built.A.t: Capture anywhere
gov.built.A.x: Record in-person meetings on the phone, or send a bot into Teams and Zoom.
gov.built.B.t: Works offline
gov.built.B.x: Recordings stay safe on the phone and sync once it is back online.
gov.built.C.t: Classification and consent
gov.built.C.x: Four classification levels and a consent confirmation before every recording.
gov.built.D.t: Live Activity
gov.built.D.x: Lock screen and Dynamic Island controls while a meeting is recording.
gov.built.E.t: One view per meeting

gov.built.F.t: Review before record
gov.built.F.x: Draft minutes a secretary reviews, edits and approves. Alerts tell people when a transcript is ready or an action lands.
