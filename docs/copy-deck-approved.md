### Wisp `/work/wisp`

**Title:** Wisp Meeting App Design & Development | Anneal Studio

**Meta description:** An iOS meeting-records app designed and built around continuous recording, classification, offline sync and review before minutes become the official record.

**H1:** Record the meeting. Review the record.

**Lead:** An iOS experience that carries a meeting from capture to approved minutes, with recording controls that remain available when the phone is locked.

**Project details:** Client: Confidential. / Sector: Meeting records. / Platform: iOS. / Contribution: Product design and the full mobile build. / Year: 2026.

**The problem**

Boards, committees and leadership groups need a dependable record of their decisions. Capturing the audio is one part of that. The product also has to respect classification, make recording clear to participants and support review before a draft becomes official.

We designed and built an app around that full workflow, including the moments when the screen locks, a connection drops or the meeting moves between tools.

**Keep capture within reach**

Recording continues when the user locks the phone or switches apps. A Live Activity shows the timer and recording status on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island. Expanded controls let the user pause, resume or stop without returning to the main app.

The recording state stays consistent across the interface.

**Make the recording process clear from the start**

Before recording, the user assigns a classification and confirms that the people present have been told about the recording. Public, Internal, Confidential and Restricted are explicit choices in the flow.

The flow makes those choices explicit before recording begins.

**Follow one meeting through**

Start with its classification and recording confirmation. Capture the meeting, including while the phone is locked. Save the recording and upload it to the organisation's servers when a connection is available. Review the transcript, actions and draft minutes together. A secretary can edit and approve the minutes before they become the official record.

**What the product includes**

Phone recording and meeting capture through Teams or Zoom; offline recording with later sync; lock-screen and Dynamic Island controls; speaker-labelled transcripts; actions with owners; minutes with a review and approval step; notifications when related work is ready.

**Built with:** React Native / iOS Live Activities / Dynamic Island / Offline sync

### Naira to crypto exchange `/work/naira-crypto-exchange`

**Title:** Crypto Exchange Product Design & Development | Anneal Studio

**Meta description:** Product strategy, design and mobile development for a confidential Nigerian fintech, connecting verification, rate confirmation, trading and naira payouts.

**H1:** A trading flow built around clear responsibilities.

**Lead:** A mobile exchange experience for buying and selling crypto against naira, with verification, trade confirmation and payouts connected in one flow.

**Project details:** Client: Confidential fintech, Nigeria. / Sector: Digital assets and payments. / Platform: iOS and Android. / Contribution: Product strategy, design and the full mobile build. / Year: 2026.

**The problem**

The founders wanted customers to buy and sell crypto on their phones. Delivering that experience meant looking beyond the trade screen. Identity checks, provider responsibilities, publishing requirements and the path from a confirmed trade to a payout all affected the product.

We mapped those dependencies before designing the main flow, so the interface reflected how the service was intended to operate.

**Begin with who is responsible**

The product connected to a partner's services. We separated the work performed through that integration from responsibilities that remained with the client. Access to an API was treated as a technical connection, rather than an assumption about the client's permissions.

Publishing requirements were considered alongside the product, including the intended entity and developer account behind the listing.

**Make the first trade understandable**

Verification comes before trading. The flow explains the required BVN and identity checks and shows progress as the customer moves through them.

For a trade, the customer sees the quoted rate and the time available to confirm it. Rate confirmation, status and receipts help explain what is happening rather than leaving the customer to infer it from a changing balance.

**Connect the transaction to its outcome**

The experience includes buying and selling, payouts to a verified bank account, trade history and individual receipts.

**What the work demonstrates**

Product strategy that accounts for external dependencies; a mobile interface connecting verification and transactions; integration with the partner API; and a clear distinction between a technical implementation and the client's regulatory responsibilities.

**Built with:** React Native / TypeScript / Node.js / Partner API

### Jewellery marketplace `/work/jewellery-marketplace`

**Title:** Jewellery Marketplace Design & Development | Anneal Studio

**Meta description:** A mobile and web marketplace with buyer offers, seller counters and a card-authorisation flow that captures payment when an offer is accepted.

**H1:** Make an offer. Pay when the seller accepts.

**Lead:** A marketplace experience that gives buyers room to negotiate and sellers a clear decision, with the payment state connected to the offer.

**Project details:** Client: Confidential jewellery marketplace. / Sector: Marketplace and payments. / Platform: iOS, Android and web. / Contribution: Payments design, product design and the full build.

**The problem**

Buyers wanted to offer a price rather than pay the listed amount. Sellers needed to accept, decline or counter and understand what would happen to payment after their decision.

We designed the offer flow and payment mechanism together. The product needed a clear state for both parties while an offer was waiting for a response.

**Separate authorisation from capture**

A card can be authorised before a payment is captured. The flow uses that distinction: the buyer confirms the card when making an offer, and acceptance triggers capture.

The flow uses card authorisation and capture rather than a custom escrow service. Provider authorisation windows and payment states shaped the offer-expiry design.

**Keep every offer state visible**

The buyer chooses an amount and submits the offer. The card is authorised and the seller receives the request. The seller can accept, decline or counter before expiry. Acceptance captures payment; a declined or expired offer triggers release of the authorisation through the payment flow.

The interface explains whether an offer is waiting, accepted or expired. It distinguishes an authorised amount from a captured payment.

**Design both sides of the negotiation**

Buyers can follow their offers and responses. Sellers can review incoming offers in one place and act on them. The same product rules carry across mobile and web.

**What the work demonstrates**

A payment mechanism chosen alongside the customer experience; buyer and seller workflows; offer expiry; counteroffers; and a shared marketplace across iOS, Android and web.

**Built with:** React Native / Next.js / Stripe Checkout
