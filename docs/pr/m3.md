### Kit additions

- `ScrollSpyNav`: marks the current section with `aria-current="location"`.
- `EmailCapture`:
  - takes any Server Action
  - shows the success panel only when the action reports the address was stored
  - moves focus to that panel and announces it through a live region

### Waitlist

- A `joinWaitlist` Server Action with Zod validation.
- A pluggable store. There is no real store until M5.
  - With `WAITLIST_STORE=memory` (tests and local), addresses are kept in memory.
  - Otherwise, every submission reports failure. The form never claims an address was saved when it wasn't.

### Copy

- Services, Products and Studio copy comes from the deck.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Deck over board where they differ:**
   - the Studio lead
   - the waitlist note and confirmation
   - "More when it is ready." on Clekey
2. **Dollar amounts in deck sentences follow the visitor's currency** (FAQ answers, add-ons, sprint), extending what the board already does for add-ons.
3. **AA lifts.** Where the board's greys fail AA, I used the closest passing value:
   - Services index numbers
   - row numbers on light surfaces
   - inactive process rows
   - the Growth "05", at full strength instead of 30%
   - the contents numbers on Privacy
4. **Heroes animate on load,** not on arrival. After a "Work with us" transition into an inner page, the hero's entrance plays under the overlay rather than after it lifts.
