# M2: Home, every section

## What changed

The `/` route now builds every HomeV6 section from the M1 kit:

- **Preloader.** The four-cell logo counts to 100, splits, and each cell flies into a hero tile (one lands in the header mark). An inline script decides before first paint. It runs once per session, only with full motion, never over a "Work with us" arrival, and never on client-side navigation. The hero text paints under the overlay from the first frame, so LCP is not delayed. A CSS failsafe uncovers the page after 6s if the script never runs.
- **Hero.**
  - Rows reveal after the preloader.
  - On scroll, the rows drift apart and the hero scales back, fades and blurs.
  - Morphing tiles, labels, the typing line, the lead, and the call-to-action buttons.

## Decisions and deviations from the board

1. **Preloader once per session.** The board plays it on every load. Return visits within a session show the hero in its final state, without the row reveal.
2. **Contrast.** Where the board fails WCAG AA, I used the closest passing value:
   - the case index counters ("01/03")
   - the "Coming soon" tag on the violet tile: a light wash instead of a dark one
   - the inactive principles, which use the M1 dim token
3. **Hero footer on phones.** "Lagos · Barcelona" is hidden below 640px so the row doesn't wrap into three ragged lines.
4. **Case cards are single links** whose accessible name is their visible content (WCAG 2.5.3). Mockups are hidden from assistive tech and excluded from axe, because WCAG 1.4.3 exempts text in images of UI.
5. **"Centiiv" and "Clekey" clip in their narrow tiles at 1440,** as in the board's geometry. They show in full when the tile widens. Is that intended?
6. **"/ month" wraps under the price** in the Growth cards at desktop widths instead of overflowing.

## Risks

- **TBT headroom.** Home's TBT is 120 to 188ms against a 200ms budget. M3 and M4 add more client components, so watch it, and lazy-mount mockups below the fold if needed.
- **Case links don't resolve yet.** They point to routes M4 builds (`/work/...`). They 404 until then.
