### Case pages (`/work/[slug]`)

- Each case is an MDX file in `apps/anneal/content/cases/`, written with a small set of components from `components/case` (`CaseHero`, `Brief`, `Questions`, `WispFlow`, `MoneyFlow`, `Principle`, `Features`, `BuiltWith`, `Next`, plus one stage per case).
- The copy sits in the MDX, word for word. A test parses every file and checks each line against the deck and the appendix.
- `content/cases/index.ts` is the typed registry: slug, title, kicker, crumb, colours, closing message and loader. The Work page, next-case links and metadata read it without loading the MDX.
- Pages are prerendered with `generateStaticParams`; unknown slugs 404.
- **Wisp:**

  - the playable capture flow: five steps, seven stages, one phone whose Dynamic Island morphs between idle, compact and expanded

- **Jewellery:**

### Cover-to-case transition

- Clicking a cover, or the next-case card, morphs it into the destination's hero stage. The case opens at the top.
- Uses the View Transitions API. Without it, a solid overlay in the cover's colour flies to the stage's box and fades. It is tested on its own by removing the API.
- Reduced motion and modified clicks navigate normally.

## Decisions and deviations

1. **Work list columns.** The board's "kind" and "platform" columns show the deck's `work.rows.*.kicker` and `work.rows.*.line`, which are the matching slots. The year column is board-only.
2. **Wisp autoplay.**
   - The flow pauses while the pointer or focus is inside it.
   - It stops for good once a step is picked, and waits while off screen.
   - The board keeps playing after a pick. Stopping gives a way to halt the moving content (WCAG 2.2.2) without a pause button, which has no copy.
3. **Next-case label.** Wisp's link reads "Next project · 02 · Fintech", the form the other two cases use. The deck's `gov.next` ("Next project: Naira to crypto exchange") is not shown.
4. **Jewellery headline on three lines**, as on the board. The appendix writes it on two; the words are the same.
5. **AA lifts.** These numbers use `#8A8A8A`, the closest grey that passes 3:1:
   - inactive Wisp step numbers (board `#BDBDBD`)
   - upcoming money flow step numbers (board `#D0D0D0`)

   Inactive money flow text uses the site's dim grey, as in M1.
6. **Small screens.** The boards have no mobile layout for the stages and covers. Their artwork keeps its desktop composition and is scaled to fit. The island states stack, each with its label.
